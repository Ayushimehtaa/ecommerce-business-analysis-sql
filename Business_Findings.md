# Business Findings and Recommendations

**Project:** E-Commerce Business Analysis Using SQL  
**Dataset:** 5,000-row sample of e-commerce orders

## 1. Customer type
**Finding:** Loyal customers generated **$5,924,892.24** in net sales, compared with **$390,076.87** from Returning customers and **$8,501.10** from New customers.

**Recommendation:** Investigate what distinguishes loyal customers and test retention initiatives. Review ways to encourage new and returning customers to purchase again.

## 2. Customer segment
**Finding:** Consumer customers generated the most net sales (**$3,355,595.67**), followed by Premium (**$1,665,493.15**), VIP (**$661,495.49**), and Business (**$640,885.90**).

**Recommendation:** Understand what drives Consumer sales and assess opportunities to grow other segments. Compare profitability as well as sales before shifting investment.

## 3. Regional sales
**Finding:** South had the highest net sales (**$843,695.73**), followed by Central (**$562,662.22**), West (**$542,988.53**), North (**$422,410.94**), and East (**$374,062.01**).

**Recommendation:** Investigate what drives sales in the South and compare product mix, customer mix, and marketing activity across regions. The highest-sales region is not necessarily the most profitable.

## 4. Sales channels
**Finding:** Mobile App generated the most net sales (**$2,506,218.99**), followed by Website (**$2,235,029.72**), Marketplace (**$988,874.42**), and Social Media (**$593,347.08**).

**Recommendation:** Evaluate Mobile App and Website using conversion rates, customer experience, acquisition costs, and profit. Investigate lower sales on Marketplace and Social Media before deciding whether to invest more.

## 5. Order outcomes
**Finding:** The sample contains **4,102 Completed**, **331 Cancelled**, **329 Returned**, and **238 Pending** orders.

**Recommendation:** Review cancellation and return reasons and the age of pending orders. Identify preventable issues and monitor whether operational changes improve completion rates.

## 6. Overall sales
**Finding:** Total net sales across the 5,000-row sample were **$6,323,470.21**. Sales from Completed orders were **$5,160,806.25**.

**Recommendation:** Use these totals as a baseline and analyze order status, region, customer group, and channel together to understand where sales are generated and where operations may improve.

## Scope note
These results describe a 5,000-row sample. Validate findings against the full dataset before presenting them as representative of all orders. The analysis identifies patterns and does not prove causation.
